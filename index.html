ABOUT LIVERPOOL FOOTBALL CLUB

Founded in 1892 in Liverpool, England, Liverpool Football Club is one of the world's most historic and successful football institutions. Known for its passionate supporters, legendary Anfield Stadium, and iconic red kits, the Reds continue to forge greatness on European and global stages.

With an unwavering spirit of togetherness, tactical brilliance, and world-class squad depth, the 2026-27 campaign represents a thrilling new era under top management.
